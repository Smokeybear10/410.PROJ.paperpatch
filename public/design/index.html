PaperPatch / Design exploration

Eight ways to review
a paper correction.

Each direction shows the same decision: a cable count was 8 when printed, changed to 10 in Excel, and says 12 on paper. The design should make that conflict easy to understand and deliberate to resolve.

These are interactive visual mockups, not the working app. Data and handwriting are illustrative. No direction has been approved by the user.
